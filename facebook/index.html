about developer

Founded in 2012, Tribeca is India’s premier luxury real estate developer, renowned for creating iconic landmarks that redefine urban skylines. Our vision is to craft the most coveted “Trophy Property” in every market we enter. As the largest developer of Trump-branded residences globally, we proudly present India’s finest and most expansive collection of luxury branded homes. Recognized for our exceptional achievements, we are the largest developer of Trump properties worldwide, the creators of the world’s largest rooftop terrace, India’s leading branded luxury developer, and the developers behind Pune’s largest high street retail destination.

With a joint legacy spanning over 18 million sq. ft. of development, VSGL is redefining urban living through thoughtful planning, sustainable design and a deep commitment to quality. Their projects across residential, commercial and industrial segments are designed to build more than spaces - they create communities that inspire growth, belonging and lasting value.

Rooted in Pune's legacy and driven by a decade of trust, Kalash Properties has redefined urban living since 2016. Blending craftsmanship, integrity and vision, every project stands as a testament to design excellence and lasting relationships shaping Pune's skyline with purpose and pride.

For over four decades, Veraco Grroup of Compaanies has delivered landmark façades where precision engineering meets architectural artistry.
